About

About VSC Projects

VSC Projects (Pty) Ltd is a multidisciplinary contracting and project services company providing electrical, construction, civil and site, plumbing and maintenance services.

Electrical and technical services form part of the company's core technical foundation, while the wider business is structured to support projects requiring multiple disciplines.
